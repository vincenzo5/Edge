# Synthesis — what Wolves Discord teaches beyond BBR mechanics

Answers the research question: *Does Banks talk about broader market context, sectors, and non-price data?*

## Short answer

**Yes on context; light on sectors; calendar + volume as non-price; flow bots exist but are not his core process.**

## 1. Broader market context — yes

Banks regularly posts outside pure entry mechanics:

| Layer | Where | What |
|-------|--------|------|
| Weekly regime | `#banks` GAME PLAN | Range / ATH fail / demand hold / make-or-break; HTF bias |
| Index structure | `#banks` SPY/QQQ/IWM ladders | Relative strength, imbalances, Weekly/Daily EMAs |
| Event regime | `#banks`, GAMEPLANs, announcements | FOMC, Fed blackout, earnings season → **do less** / day off |
| Intraday tape | Morning Watch video | “Market for the day” (video; text not scraped) |
| Patience cues | announcements | Inside day = zero trend; print then stop |

He analyzes by **zooming out** (monthly → weekly → daily), naming the range, stating whether to press or stand down, then mapping BBR levels.

## 2. Sectors — light, not a rotation desk

Observed:

- Semis rotation notes (NVDA holding while others soft)
- IWM as **rate-sensitive** into FOMC
- Mag7 treated as the working “sector” universe for day trades
- Occasional narrative color (AAPL Capex chatter, META “MOAT”)
- Sunday Watch (YT) has called XLF / financials strongest when tape is sideways

**Not observed:** systematic sector models, breadth dashboards, or sector ETF playbooks as daily process.

## 3. Non-price information

### Banks actually uses

1. **Earnings calendar** — know times; do not gamble ER  
2. **Economic / Fed calendar** — FOMC, PCE, GDP, PMI, speakers, blackout  
3. **Futures** — ES/NQ levels for context  
4. **Volume / momentum** — confirmation with BBR (taught; live chat implies via reaction)  
5. Light **narrative** (news/Capex/rotation) as color on a chart read  

### Available in Discord but optional (Tradytics)

Options flow, GEX, darkpool, sweeps, insider, analyst grades, news bots, stock breakouts — documented at tradytics.com/discord-bots; **not** the thesis source in Banks GAMEPLAN samples.

## 4. Two desks, two contexts

| | Banks day desk | EP swing desk |
|--|----------------|---------------|
| Context focus | Index regime + Mag7 + event calendar | Market chop vs trend; RS leaders |
| Stand-down | Inside day, FOMC day-off, Friday rules | “Go Fishing,” raise cash, NO SWINGS |
| Instrument | Near-dated options on indices/Mag7 | Commons + STS/LTS; some LEAPS |

## 5. Durable process diagram

```text

        weekly MARKET OVERVIEW + SPY/QQQ/IWM ladders

```
