## EP trade process (`ep-trades`)

1. Multi-timeframe thesis (M/W/D) with levels, SMAs, invalidation
2. Ticket: buy stop, targets, stop (close below), optional weekly call
3. Instruments: commons + **STS/LTS** (short/long-term swing options)
4. Manage with % trims → runners → BE stops
5. ER dates as timing context; sometimes hold through (see Paulongerrr `$MO`)
