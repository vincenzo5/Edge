# Market Wizards — standout results

Research notes on the most impressive performance claims across Jack Schwager’s *Market Wizards* series. Compiled from public summaries, interviews, and publisher blurbs (2026-08-03).

**Not investment advice.** Figures are often self-reported, estimated, or withdrawal-adjusted. Prop-firm P&L is not the same as personal-account compounding. Small-account percentage returns rarely scale linearly.

| Lens | Best candidates |

## 5. Caveats

- Self-reported / estimated figures are common; not all are audited fund track records.
- Prop desk or firm P&L (e.g. Lipschutz) ≠ personal account compounding from a $X stake.
- Withdrawals, leverage, and notional account sizing change the story (e.g. Seykota withdrawal-adjusted %, Netto notional levels).
- Small-account percentage returns do not scale; capacity and liquidity change the game.
- Survivorship bias: the series interviews winners who kept trading long enough to be interviewed.
